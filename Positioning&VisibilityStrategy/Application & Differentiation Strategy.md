# **Part 3 — Application & Differentiation Strategy**

Your resume clears filters. The problem is that at the senior level, everyone who makes it to an interview has a strong resume. You need to differentiate before the panel stage, not during it.

## **Your Positioning Statement**

| Core Positioning — Use This Consistently Across All Channels "I design AI-first delivery systems for digital consultancies — where human judgment drives decisions, AI removes execution friction, and knowledge compounds across projects instead of resetting every time." This positions you above 'experienced delivery manager' and into 'delivery system architect' — which is exactly the gap the Head of Delivery role exists to fill. |
| :---- |

## **The Orium-Gluo Acquisition Story**

Frame it explicitly in every interview:

* 'I was a Senior Delivery Manager at Gluo when Orium acquired us in \[year\]. I didn't just survive the acquisition — I led delivery continuity through it. That's direct experience managing delivery systems through organizational change, which is rare.'

This turns a potential flag into a differentiator.

## **Tailoring Your Application by Stage**

| Stage | What They Are Evaluating | Your Action |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Application / Resume | Does this person have the right background? | Your current resume works. Add one line to your summary: 'Specializing in AI-first delivery system design for digital consultancies.' Quantify Orium ART impact if possible. |
| Recruiter Screen (Step 1\) | Will this person be a fit culturally and in English? | Prepare a 90-second pitch using the positioning statement above. Speak slowly and clearly. Ask one smart question about their delivery model. |
| Technical / Panel (Steps 2-3) | Can this person think at a systems level? | Prepare 3 STAR stories mapped to: (1) AI-first delivery design, (2) cross-functional system standardization, (3) stakeholder alignment under ambiguity. Use frameworks, not just outcomes. |
| CEO / Final Stage | Is this the person I want owning delivery at this company? | Before the call, research the CEO's public content — LinkedIn posts, interviews, podcasts. Mirror their language. Ask: 'What does success look like in the first 90 days?' and 'What's the one thing about your current delivery model you most want to change?' |

## **The Psychometric Test — What to Know**

Without knowing which specific test was used in that process, I cannot tell you exactly what was measured. However, most psychometric tests used in senior leadership hiring assess some combination of:

* Problem-solving and cognitive flexibility

* Leadership and influence style

* Risk tolerance and decision-making preferences

* Emotional regulation and resilience

*Recommendation: Before your next psychometric test, ask the recruiter which tool they use (e.g., Hogan, Predictive Index, Korn Ferry, etc.). Each has publicly available preparation resources. Being aware of your own profile — not to game the test, but to understand how you naturally show up — reduces anxiety and inconsistency.*
