# **Part 4 — Outbound & Network Activation**

This is the highest-leverage area you are currently underutilizing. Senior roles at digital consultancies are frequently filled through networks before they are posted. Your goal is to be in those conversations.

## **Network Activation — Your Existing Contacts**

You have direct connections at US/Canadian companies. Here is how to activate them without asking for a job directly:

1. **Action:** Map your contacts.

List every US/Canada connection by company. Note their seniority and whether they are in delivery, product, or leadership. Prioritize anyone at Director level or above.

2. **Action:** Send a warm 'advice ask' message — not a job ask.

Example message (adapt to your voice):

| Template — Warm Network Outreach Hi \[Name\], I hope you're doing well. I've been expanding my focus toward Head of Delivery roles at digital consultancies in the US and Canada, and I'd value a 20-minute conversation with someone who knows the market from the inside. I'm not asking for a referral — I'm genuinely trying to understand how delivery leadership is evolving at companies like \[theirs\]. Would you be open to a quick call in the next couple of weeks? Either way, let's stay in touch. \[Your name\] |
| :---- |

3. **Action:** Ask one specific question per conversation.

'Who else in your network do you think I should talk to?' is the highest-value question. One warm introduction is worth 20 cold applications.

## **Cold Outreach — Decision Makers at Target Companies**

For Tier 1 companies where you have no existing connection, use a direct but non-generic outreach approach on LinkedIn:

| Template — Cold LinkedIn Message to VP/Director of Delivery Hi \[Name\], I've been following \[Company\]'s work in composable commerce — the \[specific project or article\] caught my attention. I'm a Senior Delivery Manager with 9+ years in digital consultancy, currently building toward a Head of Delivery role. I specialize in designing AI-first delivery systems — where human judgment drives decisions and knowledge compounds across projects. I'd value 15 minutes to learn how \[Company\] thinks about delivery leadership at scale. Not pitching a role — genuinely curious about your model. Worth a conversation? — Pedro |
| :---- |

Key principles for cold outreach:

* Reference something specific about their company — not generic praise

* Lead with your value positioning, not your job title

* Make the ask small (15 minutes, not 'any open roles?')

* Send a maximum of 5-7 of these per week — quality over volume

## **Weekly Outbound Target**

| Activity | Weekly Target |
| :---- | :---- |
| Warm network messages (existing contacts) | 2–3 per week |
| Cold LinkedIn messages to decision-makers at Tier 1 companies | 3–5 per week |
| New applications (targeted, not spray-and-pray) | 5–8 per week (down from 10, but higher quality) |
| LinkedIn engagement on target company posts | Daily — comment meaningfully on 2-3 posts |
| Company career page checks (Tier 1 list) | 2x per week |
