# **Part 2 — Target Company Strategy**

Stop searching reactively. The goal is to build a curated list of 20-30 companies that match your profile, then pursue them proactively — whether or not they have an open role.

## **Your Ideal Company Profile**

| Criteria | Description |
| :---- | :---- |
| Type | Mid-size digital consultancy or systems integrator (50–500 employees) |
| Geography | Headquartered in USA or Canada; actively hiring remote LATAM talent |
| Focus | Composable commerce, digital transformation, AI consulting, platform engineering |
| Stage | Growth-stage: post-Series A or private equity-backed; building delivery infrastructure |
| Delivery model | Client-facing project teams; delivery manager role has P\&L or margin visibility |
| LATAM posture | Has distributed teams or has hired LATAM senior leaders before |

## **Where to Search — Beyond LinkedIn**

| Platform | How to Use It |
| :---- | :---- |
| Himalayas.app | Best for remote-first companies; filter by 'Head of Delivery' or 'Director of Delivery'; many AI-first consultancies post here |
| Wellfound (AngelList) | Startup and scale-up roles; good for growth-stage consultancies with LATAM hiring |
| Builtin.com | US tech companies; filter by remote \+ delivery leadership |
| MACH Alliance jobs board | Niche but high-signal; composable commerce companies only |
| LinkedIn — Boolean search | Use: ("Head of Delivery" OR "Director of Delivery" OR "VP Delivery") AND (consulting OR consultancy OR agency) AND remote |
| Company career pages | For Tier 1 companies, check careers pages directly every 2 weeks — senior roles often bypass job boards |
